# Heroteaser Unit 15

- Werkzeug: integriertes `image_gen` (Bildgenerierung).
- Webdatei: `docs/assets/images/unit15/hero-unit15.jpg`.
- Webformat: 2400 × 400 Pixel, JPEG, Qualität 90; proportional skaliert und mittig auf das gemeinsame Bannerformat der Units 09–14 zugeschnitten.
- Verwendung: alle drei Unit-15-Seiten, mit deutscher Bildbeschreibung und Kennzeichnung als KI-generierte Illustration.
- Das Motiv ist eine illustrative Landschaft und stellt keine vermessene Karte oder Kursdaten dar.

## Verwendeter Prompt

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: panoramic website hero for Unit 15, the final recap of a German university introductory data and GIS course.
Primary request: Create one sophisticated cinematic photographic illustration summarizing the journey from structured tabular data and charts to spatial data and a finished map. Match the visual language of a series of dark, muted photographic aerial-landscape GIS banners: deep navy shadows, forest green, teal river water, warm pale gold highlights, delicate translucent cartographic overlays, realistic terrain.
Scene/backdrop: A continuous oblique aerial panorama of a central European wooded river valley, fields and small settlements, with a gently winding river visually connecting the entire composition.
Subject: On the left, understated translucent tabular rows and a simple small scatterplot without text blend into the terrain; across the centre, a few warm small observation dots and fine polygon outlines connect data to the landscape; towards the right the landscape subtly resolves into shaded-relief cartography with a fine raster patch and contour lines. A coherent reflective overview of completed course work, calm and visually polished.
Composition/framing: Extra-wide 6:1 banner, target 2400 x 400 pixels. Full bleed continuous panorama. Dark quiet left quarter, principal details in middle and right, all essential subjects contained in the middle horizontal third if a taller canvas is unavoidable. No horizon or large sky, no separate panels, no hard collage edges.
Lighting/mood: Soft atmospheric light, subtle haze and depth, restrained contrast matching dark navy aerial-image headers, warm accents suggesting a thoughtful course conclusion.
Constraints: No words, letters, numbers, title, logos, watermarks, people, graduation caps, computer screens or UI. Tables and chart must be subtle abstract marks, not factual teaching data. Terrain is illustrative, not a purported accurate map of a named location. Create a new image, not a copy of an existing banner.
```
